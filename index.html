Services

Custom AI agents for your business.

We build scoped agents that answer from your own data, take real actions inside your tools, and hand off to a person when they should. One job each, measured, and shipped as a service you own.

  • SUPPORT

    Customer support agent

    Answers product questions from your own documentation, escalating to a human when the answer is not in the source.

  • SALES

    Sales qualification agent

    Qualifies inbound leads against your criteria, captures the details that matter, and routes the rest.

  • OPS

    Internal operations agent

    Reads your internal tools and turns routine requests into completed actions with an audit trail.

  • KNOWLEDGE

    Knowledge agent

    Puts a searchable, cited layer over procedures, contracts and past work so answers stop living in one person's head.

scope → ground → integrate → hand over. One job per agent, bound to your own sources.
